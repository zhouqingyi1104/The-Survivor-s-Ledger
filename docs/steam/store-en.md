# The Survivor's Ledger · English store page

## Game title

The Survivor's Ledger

## Short description

Spend three days stocking a courtyard before the dead descend on Linchuan. Scavenge by lantern, keep your promises to the neighbors, and decide which breach to hold at night. What you leave unfinished may follow you into another life. A single-player survival sim with Simplified Chinese text.

## About this game

The rice shop closes at noon on the third day. The builders need your order even sooner. One more sack of grain could cost you the cart that would carry home enough firewood. When the plague reaches Linchuan, you return to your courtyard with whatever you managed to save.

Once the streets fall quiet, there's water to draw, dinner to cook, and a neighbor waiting for the medicine you promised. Take a lantern to the ruined pharmacy or search the lumberyard for wood. Searching costs daylight. So does the walk back. As curfew draws near, which find will you leave behind?

At night, the dead press against your gate, windows, and courtyard wall. Choose a standing watch order or direct the defense yourself. Between waves you have time for one action. Mend the window, put up a ward, or open the gate and draw your blade. Anything you let through can trample your crops or break the stove you'll need tomorrow.

A wandering Taoist leaves a trail to the mortuary and a hillside temple. Hold out, find a way to evacuate, or try to break the ritual behind the plague. If you die, open the ledger to see what brought you down. Start another life and an old gift or broken promise may surface as a case worth investigating. The game interface, story, and dialogue are in Simplified Chinese.

## Early Access questions

### Why Early Access?

We want to see how first-time players spend their twenty days in Linchuan. Do you take a detour to find medicine for a neighbor or hurry home to mend a window? Which choices give you pause, and when does one day start to feel too much like the last? Those answers will help us tune the daytime routine, the night watch, and the clues that guide an investigation.

### Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?

We don't have a date for the full release yet. We plan to collect feedback from the opening preparations through death or an ending, then test the difficulty of different strategies and the changes players encounter in a second life. We'll revisit the estimate once we have those results.

### How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?

We want a first-time player to understand where a defense failed and a returning player to find good reasons to take a different risk. We plan to focus on the pressure at each difficulty level, chores that become repetitive over twenty days, and clues that help players understand the consequences of a choice. The order of those changes will depend on where players get stuck.

### What is the current state of the Early Access version?

The current Windows build is a single-player, offline game in Simplified Chinese. Choose from four starting identities, prepare for three days, manage a courtyard, explore eight locations, and defend your home in three-wave sieges. Help your neighbors and investigate the plague to hold out, evacuate, or break the ritual. A final account records your fate, and some memories can resurface in a later life. We're still tuning daytime pacing and difficulty.

### Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?

We haven't decided whether to change the price for the full release. If a change is planned, we'll announce it on the store page.

### How are you planning on involving the community in your development process?

Tell us in the Steam discussions which difficulty you chose, what happened on which day, what you were trying to do, and what stopped you. You can export a local playtest report from Settings or the death and ending screens. It includes the seed, a recent log, and three optional feedback questions. Share whichever details you are comfortable posting. We'll use these accounts to reproduce problems and decide which part of the game to revisit first.

## Mature content description

The game depicts a fictional plague, famine, death, and sieges by the undead. The player can fight the undead with a blade. Some written scenes describe wounds, traces of blood, tending to the dead, and armed looting. The nighttime scenes may be unsettling.

## Generative AI disclosure

During development, we used generative AI to create some inventory illustrations and assist in designing some side-story events and their branches. The artwork and prewritten events ship with the game. The Windows release resolves events and player choices locally and does not call a generative AI service during play.
